14 September 2026. Delivers the three priorities from the preceding checkpoint together, within original design Phases 6/8 and their authority dependencies. These are three implementation outcomes, not three newly invented original design phases. Existing Nest/Prisma/PostgreSQL and Angular/PrimeNG patterns remain; no dependencies were added.

## Delivered behavior

1. Higher-authority reopening requires an independent live authority strictly above the actual previous terminal decision: Use-Case Owner 0, Responsible AI Officer 1, Ethics Committee 2, AI Executive Team 3, Steering Committee 4. The previous decision, conditions and calculation remain immutable. An append-only reversal records the actual authority and previous decision; the existing reassessment pipeline creates a fresh owner coordinator and assessment round, supersedes pending monitoring, and requires fresh downstream scoring and approvals. The public seven procedure triggers remain separate from the server-derived authority trigger. Reopening does not reactivate an archived use case.
2. AVOID requires actual Risk Owner evidence and written implementation details for scope change or complete stop. The assigned independent Officer approves or returns the proposal. Return requires new evidence/proposal. Approval opens a separate closure task. Verified scope-change closure closes the risk case while preserving the approved use-case intake and other risks. Complete stop requires independent Steering closure and archives the entire use case with immutable operational provenance. This records evidence of operational implementation; it does not change frozen approved intake, certify external software changes, create residual scores or accept risk.
3. ESCALATE uses native GovernanceEscalation records and the four installed council levels, with AI-specific assigned delegates: domain council/Officer, stewardship council/Ethics, governance board/AI Executive Team, executive steering/Steering. Each current assigned live delegate can advance to the next level or resolve with a written instruction for a fresh Risk Owner response proposal and independent Officer approval. Steering cannot advance beyond the ladder. Generic executive remains aggregate-only. No council outcome fabricates residual scores, accepts risk or skips a fresh response approval.
