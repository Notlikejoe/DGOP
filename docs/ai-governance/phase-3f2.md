## Delivered packet

- Only an active assigned Risk Owner with airs.risk.assess and scoped visibility can start/restart/calculate a residual round. Its current inherent assessment must be adopted and its current response approved. MITIGATE/TRANSFER requires a real independently approved plan and all its planned actions completed. Native task state, immutable progress/completion evidence, original executor identity, plan/action membership, stage and deadline provenance must agree. High/Critical with zero completed actions is blocked by RM-24.
- ACCEPT at inherent Low/Medium can open a fresh residual round from its protected acceptance-prerequisite task without fabricating treatment. ACCEPT at inherent High/Critical is blocked by RM-24; AVOID and ESCALATE retain their separate operational paths. Returning an already approved strategy for a real plan is a later dedicated review path, not a generic workflow edit.
- Start creates one assigned residual coordinator and eight fresh competent-role tasks using current effective published R_SCORE14/R_IMPD/R_LEVEL. Actual Risk/Use-Case Owners are assigned to their mapped dimensions; other specialists use existing role queues. The active Use-Case Owner must have explicit assessment permission and scoped visibility for reputation assessment. Previous inherent scores and approvals are never copied into residual contributions.
- The coordinator also pins an effective published R_CTRLEFF choice list. Completion requires a selected governed control-effectiveness code and fresh post-treatment current-controls text, in addition to justified likelihood and eight justified 1–4 impacts. Effectiveness is a separately recorded grade; it has no invented numeric modifier.
- Residual impact is MAX of the eight fresh scores, residual score is likelihood × impact, and the existing governed bands/tie-breaking/severity mappings apply. The immutable record retains original actor/task/reference, inherent/response/plan/completed-progress provenance and controls. Risk reduction is display-only (inherent − residual)/inherent ×100, including negative values when risk worsens.
- A changed/retired/expired scoring or control-effectiveness publication blocks contributions/calculation. Owner restart requires justification, cancels only the current open residual tasks, pins current publications and opens all eight tasks again. Earlier submitted contributions remain recorded and cannot authorize the replacement round.
- Calculation completes the coordinator and opens one protected Responsible AI Officer residual-review task. The result explicitly records adopted=false and riskAccepted=false; the case remains Under Review. Calculation cannot accept/close risk or start monitoring. A scored response cannot be rescored/restarted without a later dedicated officer-return path.
- All mutations use serializable transactions, optimistic risk versions, workflow events and required audit. Audit failure rolls back coordinator/dimension creation, claims, calculation/history, review-task routing and version changes. Concurrent duplicate starts/calculations succeed exactly once. Combined-role Auditors stay read-only; token claims and posted actor/computed/authority fields cannot confer authority.
